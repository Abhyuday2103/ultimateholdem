  • Ante and Blind are equal. Ante $10 to $500. Trips and Pairs Bonus $5 to $100, both optional.
  • Before the flop: check or bet 3x or 4x ante. After the flop: check or bet 2x. On the river: bet 1x or fold.
  • Beat the dealer: Play pays 1 to 1, Ante pays 1 to 1 (pushes if dealer does not open), Blind pays per table.
  • Trips pays on your final hand even if you fold or lose. Fresh single 52-card deck every hand.
